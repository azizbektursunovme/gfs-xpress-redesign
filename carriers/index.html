Start hauling in three steps

From application to your first load in as little as 24 to 48 hours.

  1. Apply online

    Send your MC/DOT number, insurance certificate and W-9. It takes about 10 minutes.

  2. Get verified

    Our compliance team checks your authority, insurance and safety rating, usually within 24 to 48 hours.

  3. Start hauling

    Book loads from the load board or by phone. Your carrier rep is with you from the first load.

What you need

  • Active MC/DOT operating authority for at least 6 months
  • Auto liability insurance of at least $1,000,000
  • Cargo insurance of at least $100,000
  • Satisfactory safety rating
  • W-9 and a signed carrier agreement
